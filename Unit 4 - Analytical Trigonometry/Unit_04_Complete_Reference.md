## ⚠️ 4. Strict Formatting Rules for Worksheets A, B, and C
*(Papers that DO NOT follow these requirements will be returned ungraded)*

1. **All work must be shown**: Clearly communicate your mathematical process to the reader. Answers only receive zero credit.
2. **No Calculators**: Calculators are strictly prohibited on all problems (may only be used to verify answers at home).
3. **Question Numbering**: Every problem must be numbered in the left margin so questions stand out clearly.
4. **Column Layout**: You may use two columns per page (fold the page in half to keep columns neat).
5. **No Broken Problems**: NEVER start a problem in one column or page and finish it in another.
6. **Do NOT Box or Circle Anything**: Leave the final line clean without drawing boxes or circles around answers.
7. **Left-Side Only for VTI**: When verifying identities, only manipulate the LEFT side. Always copy the original equation with equal signs aligned.
