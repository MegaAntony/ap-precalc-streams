📝 Master Formula Cheatsheet & Decision Trees
Exam Quick Reference
1. Principal Branches of Inverse Trigonometric Functions (No-Calc Day)

$$\arcsin x \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right] \quad (\text{Quadrants I and IV, where IV is negative: } [-\pi/2, 0))$$ $$\arccos x \in [0, \pi] \quad (\text{Quadrants I and II})$$ $$\arctan x \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right) \quad (\text{Quadrants I and IV, open interval})$$

2. Oblique Triangle Area Formulas (Calculator Day)

SAS Configuration (Two sides & included angle): $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C = \frac{1}{2}bc\sin A = \frac{1}{2}ac\sin B$$ SSS Configuration (Heron's Formula): $$\text{Semi-perimeter: } s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}$$ $$\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)}$$

3. Law of Cosines & Law of Sines (Calculator Day)

Law of Cosines ($SAS$ or $SSS$): $$c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos C \iff \cos C = \frac{a^2 + b^2 - c^2}{2ab}$$ ★ Pro-Tip: For SSS, ALWAYS solve for the LARGEST side's opposite angle first! That guarantees the remaining angles are strictly acute.

Law of Sines ($AAS, ASA$, or $SSA$): $$\frac{\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{b} = \frac{\sin C}{c}$$

4. Ambiguous Case ($SSA$) Decision Tree for Acute Angle $A$

Given acute angle $A$ and adjacent side $b$, first compute the altitude: $h = b\sin A$:

  • $a < h$: Opposite side is too short to reach base $\implies$ 0 Triangles (No Solution).
  • $a = h$: Opposite side drops as a perpendicular altitude $\implies$ 1 Right Triangle.
  • $h < a < b$: Opposite side can swing inward or outward $\implies$ 2 Oblique Triangles.
    • Triangle 1: $B_1 = \arcsin\left(\frac{b\sin A}{a}\right)$ (acute)
    • Triangle 2: $B_2 = 180^\circ - B_1$ (obtuse). Check: $A + B_2 < 180^\circ$.
  • $a \ge b$: Opposite side swings outward only $\implies$ 1 Oblique Triangle.

For Obtuse Angle $A$: If $a \le b \implies$ 0 Triangles. If $a > b \implies$ 1 Triangle.
