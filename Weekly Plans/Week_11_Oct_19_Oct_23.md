By the end of the week students will be able to:

* solve trig equations by factoring

* solve trig equations using trig identities

* solve trig equations by squaring both sides

* solve trig equations involving multiple angles

* write general solutions when solving trig equations

* simplify, rewrite, and evaluate trig expressions using

* sum and difference identities

* product to sum and sum to product identities

* double angle identities

* half angle identities

* power reducing identities
