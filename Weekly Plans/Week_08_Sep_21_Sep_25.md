## 🎯 Success Criteria
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
1. Solve right triangles.
2. Find the area of triangles using 2 sides and the included angle ($SAS$).
3. Find the area of triangles using all 3 sides ($SSS$ / Heron’s formula).
4. Solve an oblique (non-right) triangle using the **Law of Cosines**.
