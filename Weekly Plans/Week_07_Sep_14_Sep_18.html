🎯 Success Criteria

By the end of this week, students will be able to:
1. Perform basic inverse and composition of functions.
2. Identify the domain and range of inverse trig functions.
3. Evaluate composite trig functions using proper domain and range restrictions on arc functions.
4. Rewrite and solve trig expressions as rational expressions.
