🎯 Success Criteria & Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:
1. Solve an acute or obtuse oblique triangle using the Law of Sines.
2. Read an application word problem involving an oblique triangle, determine whether the Law of Cosines ($SAS$, $SSS$) or Law of Sines ($AAS$, $ASA$, $SSA$) applies, and solve.
3. Rapidly determine the number of possible triangles (0, 1, or 2) in an Ambiguous Case of the Law of Sines ($SSA$).
4. Systematically calculate both solutions for the 2-triangle case using Mr. O'Connor's angle-supplement method ($B_2 = 180^\circ - B_1$).
5. Solve multi-step geometric application problems blending right triangle trig and oblique triangle techniques.

📝 Unit 3 Test Readiness Checklist (Next Week: Mon 10/12 & Tue 10/13)

  • [ ] Inverse Function Basics: Finding algebraic inverses, restricted domains.
  • [ ] Arc Trig Evaluations: Evaluating $\arcsin$, $\arccos$, $\arctan$ within proper principal branches:
  • $\arcsin x \in [-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}]$ (Quadrants I & IV)
  • $\arccos x \in [0, \pi]$ (Quadrants I & II)
  • $\arctan x \in (-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2})$ (Quadrants I & IV)
  • [ ] Composite Expressions:
  • Numerical compositions (e.g. $\cos(\arcsin(-\frac{3}{5}))$)
  • Algebraic compositions using reference triangles (e.g. $\tan(\arccos(x))$)
  • [ ] Triangle Area Formulas:
  • $SAS$: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$
  • $SSS$ (Heron's): $s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}$, $\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$
  • [ ] Law of Cosines: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos C$ (and solving for angle: $\cos C = \frac{a^2 + b^2 - c^2}{2ab}$)
  • [ ] Law of Sines & SSA: Single triangle, no triangle, and two-triangle cases.
  • [ ] Trig Identities Drills: Double angle, half angle, sum & difference, and power reducing formulas.
